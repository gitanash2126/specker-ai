The Core Problem: The Wall of Hesitation

02 / 10

🧠 Why Conversational Voice Apps Fail

In apps like Duolingo, users have infinite time to think before tapping multiple-choice buttons. In a spoken voice app, an ESL learner faces "The Wall of Hesitation":

Fear of Judgement: "What if my accent or grammar is terrible?"
Blank Mind Syndrome: "What am I supposed to talk about right now?"
Action Paralysis: Too many generic categories without a 1-tap starter lead to immediate bounce.

🎯 The 3 Guiding Design Pillars

Pillar 1

Zero-Friction Launch

Cut Time-to-First-Speech to under 2 taps by providing a pre-loaded Daily Topic with Stella.

Pillar 2

Atomic Habit Mechanics

Introduce a 12-minute daily speaking goal ring + streak protection to drive D7 retention.

Pillar 3

Closed-Loop Scorecards

Turn passive metrics into immediate 5-second phonetic audio micro-drills on the home screen.

Senior UX Thinking

Edge Cases & System States

07 / 10
🌱 Day-0 New User (Cold Start)

Problem: No previous scorecards or streak exist. User feels intimidated.
Solution: Hero card adapts to: "Meet Stella: 3-Minute Friendly Diagnostic Call (No pressure, just say hello)."

🛡️ Streak Protection & Recovery

Problem: Broken streaks cause user churn.
Solution: Loss mitigation banner: "Streak frozen! Complete 5 mins today to restore your 7-day momentum."

💎 Freemium Limit & Pro Conversion

Problem: Hard paywalls cause high drop-off.
Solution: Transparent minute balance (180 mins left). Button seamlessly prompts: "Upgrade to Unlimited Stella Pro" when low.

📶 Network Latency & Intermittent Audio

Problem: Cellular drops cause confusion.
Solution: Waveform pulses amber with micro-text: "Stella is listening (Optimizing audio connection)..."

Authentic Reflection

Designer's Self-Critique & Trade-Offs

09 / 10

⚖️ Trade-offs Made

1. Single Hero vs. Catalog Breadth: I deliberately prioritized 1 curated daily topic over showing 10 categories above the fold. Hick's law dictates that excessive choices trigger hesitation.

2. Voice-First vs. Text Drills: I omitted passive vocabulary matching games from the home screen to protect Specker’s core differentiator: conversational speaking.

🔬 What I Would Test in Week 2

1. Persona-based onboarding branching: Dynamically swapping the Hero topic based on user goal (Tech interview vs. daily casual social).

2. Audio-only vs. Video avatar toggle: Testing if a visual 3D face helps or distracts anxious learners during speaking calls.
